## One-liner

Local-first client onboarding, project handoff checklists, and invoice recovery for freelancers and small agencies.

## Problem

Agencies and freelancers lose time and cash to messy kickoffs and unpaid invoices. Notion checklists and spreadsheet aging reports do not rank risk or close the handoff loop.

## Solution

ForgeHandoff is a focused workspace:

- Client registry  
- Standard handoff checklist with live score (0–100)  
- Invoice log with overdue status and recovery priority  
- Health dashboard (at-risk $ and open projects)  
- Browser demo (localStorage) + Node server for self-host
